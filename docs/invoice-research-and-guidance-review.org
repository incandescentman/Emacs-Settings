Codex judgment: The mistake was promoting a generic option into a recommendation without assessing its usefulness for your actual invoices or clearly separating the source's guidance from my choice. ** Payee identity and W-9
Source finding: The [[https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw9.pdf][IRS Form W-9 instructions]] place a sole proprietor's individual name on line 1 and the business/trade name on line 2. [[https://kippneworleans.org/procurement/][KIPP New Orleans Schools]] requires a W-9 from new vendors. [[https://www.palomar.edu/businessservices/independent-contractor-info/][Palomar College's contractor workflow]] requires a W-9 and vendor registration before its purchase-order process.

Codex recommendation: Keep Sunjay E. Dixit identified as supplier, with the appropriate brand in the letterhead. Establish whether each new school needs your W-9 or vendor onboarding, using its designated submission route. These examples establish real institutional practices, not Horace Mann's specific requirements.

** Billing destination, purchase orders, and ACH
Source finding: KIPP directs invoices to Accounts Payable, requests the purchasing contact and service address, and requires a purchase-order number when applicable. It requires a separate authorization form for ACH. Palomar directs invoices to Accounts Payable and explicitly warns that missing purchase-order numbers delay processing.

Codex recommendation: Confirm the school's actual billing destination and required reference with the person booking you. Supply its PO number if applicable; do not invent one. Do not assume that putting bank details on the invoice completes the school's ACH enrollment. Horace Mann's requirements and current vendor-registration status remain unverified; no public AP instructions were found in the targeted search.

** Payment timing
Source finding: KIPP publishes its own Net 30 minimum, demonstrating that payer procedures can specify timing. This is not evidence of Horace Mann's terms.

Codex recommendation: For future engagements, establish payment arrangements before the work. For Horace Mann, ask about its payment process and expected timing without claiming an agreed deadline exists. Keep the invoice free of fabricated payment terms.
