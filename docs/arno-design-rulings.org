#+TITLE: Arno PDF Template: Design Rulings and Preferences
#+DATE: 2026-09-27

* Scope

This is the canonical record of Jay's choices for the default Org PDF class ~arno-claude-design~, implemented in [[file+emacs:/Users/jay/emacs/emacs-settings/latex-templates/arno-approved-default.el][arno-approved-default.el]]. It is for content PDFs, such as business proposals. The EDF slide manuscript was a test document, not the template's sole purpose. These choices are specific to this class: they are not global website UI preferences and do not approve [[file+emacs:/Users/jay/emacs/emacs-settings/latex-templates/arno-claude-proposal.el][arno-claude-proposal.el]], Claude's experimental class, as a whole. The older Org class identifiers remain so existing documents continue to export.

* Standing preferences expressed in this review

- Favor generously sized, readable type and looser lines over a crowded page. Judge type by its rendered size: the condensed label's earlier ~12pt~ setting looked too small because inherited font scaling reduced it.
- Use one treatment for every heading at a given Org level. A new heading's words do not require separate style approval.
- Prefer the justified body shown in the real proposal comparison, while avoiding ugly hyphenation. This is not a request to suppress every ordinary word break.
- Compare meaningful visual changes side by side in rendered documents using the installed fonts and representative content before changing this default.

* Selected design

** Page and prose

- Use Arno Pro body type with justified paragraphs, symmetric ~1.375in~ side margins, ~\setstretch{1.22}~, and ~9pt plus 2pt minus 1pt~ between paragraphs. Keep the current, slightly wider space after sentences; do not add ~\frenchspacing~.
- Use the Arno bullet in soft ink. Top-level list items have ~3pt~ between them, with tighter indents for nested lists.
- Use ~\lefthyphenmin=3~ to avoid two-letter fragments before automatic hyphen breaks, retaining the shared five-letter right minimum. Selected on 2026-10-11 as B in both the Arno and Elegant Garamond comparisons; occasional awkward paragraph endings do not justify different defaults for the two reusable templates.

** Heading hierarchy

- Level 1 keeps the existing bold SF treatment. Level 2 uses regular Arno at ~22pt~.
- Level 3 uses regular uppercase Arno at ~15pt~, tracked ~5%~, with lining figures. This applies uniformly to headings such as “TL;DR” and “Section 1.”
- Level 4 uses a red condensed Helvetica Neue LT Pro label at a true ~10pt~ on its own line. Every level-4 heading gets this treatment, including “Key Findings,” “Speaker Notes,” and “Stage-ready findings.” Keep the label above its content with the selected ~16pt plus 3pt minus 2pt~ space before and ~-4pt~ after.
- Level 5 uses bold Arno at body size on its own line.

** Color, furniture, and page flow

- Keep the original powder-blue quote box and the original blue links (~elegantblue~, ~#4380b9~).
- Use the older running-header design with the added thin rule and the smaller ~10.1pt~ font setting. The left header defaults to the document author; an explicit ~\leftheader{...}~ overrides it.
- Keep the Arno contents title and blue page numbers. Use the selected lighter, more widely spaced gray dot leaders.
- Contents entries wrap between words. Keep an introduction with its outer bullet or numbered list while retaining ordinary page breaks within nested lists. Tested on the real EDF excerpt on 2026-10-11, preserving its 15-page length.
- Keep headings with the text they introduce. Let level-1 headings flow naturally rather than forcing each onto a new page. Export through ~latexmk~ so contents page numbers settle; the shared prose defaults already handle widow and orphan lines.

* Rejected or superseded treatments

- The pink ~#FBB6CE~ link color was tried and then explicitly reversed to the original blue.
- The stone-rose quote panel, ragged-right body sample, equal spacing after periods, and margin-placed red labels were not selected.
- The ~1.625in~ side margins were passed over for the selected ~1.375in~ general PDF measure.
- The earlier ~18pt~ level-3 heading and ~12pt~ level-4 setting were superseded by the rendered ~15pt~ Arno heading and true-size ~10pt~ condensed label. The former SF level-5 treatment was superseded by bold Arno at body size.
- The large SF contents title and Claude's per-document ~\PartsOnNewPage~ option were not selected. That option would break before every level-1 heading, leaving large blank areas in the tested manuscript.
