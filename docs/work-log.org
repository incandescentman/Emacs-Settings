* 2026-05-19: Teach =whittle-transcript= to clean comma-laden false starts and em-dash interruptions

* 2026-05-03: Two follow-up fixes for the Fountain =s-p= export
- *Bug 1 — keymap precedence:* the original =s-p= binding lived in =my/fountain-mode-bindings= and was installed into =fountain-mode-map= via =my/install-mode-bindings=. Reverted =my/fountain-mode-bindings= back to plain =my/writing-mode-bindings= since the =s-p= entry there was dead code; replaced with a docstring note pointing at the override map so the next reader doesn't repeat the mistake.
